### Hybrid Focus (Habits + Tasks)

```
ChainHabit - The All-in-One Habit Tracker & Todo List

Stop switching between multiple apps. ChainHabit combines the power of long-term habit building with a simple, effective daily todo list to help you own your day.

✓ HABITS & TODOS IN ONE PLACE
Manage your recurring daily routines alongside your one-time tasks. Whether it's "Mediate for 10 minutes" (Habit) or "Call the plumber" (Todo), ChainHabit handles it all in a beautiful, unified interface.

PERFECT FOR:
• Building morning routines

The person you want to become starts with the habits you build and the tasks you complete today. 

Download ChainHabit now and start building your chain!
```

## App Store Review Notes (For Apple)

```
Hello Apple Review Team,

Thank you for reviewing HabitTracker.

TESTING INSTRUCTIONS:
1. App opens to empty state on first launch
2. Tap (+) to add a habit
3. Enter name, select frequency and color
4. Tap Save to create habit
5. Tap circle icon to mark habit complete
6. Tap habit row to see detail view with calendar
7. All data is stored locally using SwiftData

NO SIGN-IN REQUIRED:
This app works 100% offline with local storage only.

EXPORT COMPLIANCE:
App does not use encryption beyond standard iOS data protection.

Thank you!
```
