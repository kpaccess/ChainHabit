Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2026

TL;DR: HabitTracker does not collect, store, or share any of your personal data. Everything stays on your device. Period.

Introduction

HabitTracker ("the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your information.

Data Collection

We do not collect any data. The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data.

Data Storage

All data created in HabitTracker is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This includes:

  • Habit names you create
  • Habit colors and frequencies
  • Completion logs and dates
  • Any other information you enter in the app

This data never leaves your device. It is not backed up to any cloud service, sent to any servers, or shared with any third parties.

Third-Party Services

HabitTracker does not use any third-party services, analytics, advertising networks, or tracking tools. There are no third-party SDKs integrated into the app.

Analytics and Tracking

The app does not include any analytics or tracking code. We do not track:

  • How you use the app
  • What habits you create
  • When you open or close the app
  • Your device information
  • Your location

iCloud Sync

Currently, HabitTracker does not support iCloud sync. All data remains on your device only. If you delete the app, all data will be permanently deleted.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13. Since no data is collected at all, the app is safe for all ages.

Data Security

Your data is as secure as your device. We recommend:

  • Keeping your device locked with a passcode or biometric authentication
  • Keeping your iOS software up to date
  • Being aware that if someone has access to your unlocked device, they can access the app

Your Rights

Since we don't collect any data, there is no data for us to:

  • Access
  • Modify
  • Delete
  • Export

You have complete control over your data at all times because it never leaves your device. You can delete all data by deleting the app.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we make any changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We will also notify users through an app update if there are any material changes to how we handle data.

International Users

Because no data is collected or transmitted, this app complies with privacy regulations worldwide, including GDPR (Europe), CCPA (California), and other data protection laws.
